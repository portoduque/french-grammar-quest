Verbs with spelling changes
Verbs ending in -ger include the -e- as part of the stem with endings that begin with the
letter a or o. Examples: manger (nous mangeons), plonger (il plongeait), ronger (il
rongea), arranger (nous arrangeâmes).
   Verbs ending in -er change the c to ç when endings begin with the letter a or o.
   Examples: commencer (nous commençons), lancer (elle lança).
