         Il ne s’agit pas de plaisanter.                This is no time for jokes.
         Il s’agit de faire vite.                 We must act quickly.
         Il ne s’agit pas de dettes.                            It is not a question of debts.
         Il s’agit d’amour.                                      It is a matter of love.

Verbs ending in -eindre and -aindre
Among the -re verbs, some verbs ending in -eindre or -aindre can be grouped
together.
   Verbs like peindre (to paint) include teindre (to dye), ceindre (to encircle),
feindre  (to  feign), craindre  (to  fear), plaindre  (to  pity), and se plaindre  (to
complain).
   Let’s conjugate the verb peindre:

        je peins            I paint            nous peignons         we paint
        tu peins        you paint         vous peignez           you paint
         il/elle peint      he/she paints         ils/elles peignent         they paint

      Le peintre peint le plafond de     The painter is painting the ceiling of the
           l’opéra.                           opera.
      Vous peignez un tableau abstrait.   You are painting an abstract painting.

  Craindre follows a similar pattern.

        je crains             I fear           nous craignons         we fear
        tu crains         you fear         vous craignez            you fear
         il/elle craint       he/she fears        ils/elles craignent          they fear

         Ils craignent le pire.                  They fear the worst.
          Il ne craint pas la douleur.           He is not afraid of pain.
