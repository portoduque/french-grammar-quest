Il y a (there is, there are)
Il y a is an impersonal expression that means both there is and there are.

         Il y a un chat sur le canapé.          There is a cat on the sofa.
         Il n’y a plus rien dans le frigo.       There is nothing left in the fridge.

    Il y a is used in a variety of expressions.

       Qu’est-ce qu’il y a?                 What’s the matter?
         Il n’y a qu’à leur dire.                Just tell them.
         Il y avait une fois...               Once upon a time . . .
         Il y a cinquante kilomètres d’ici à       It’s fifty kilometers from here to Paris.

Il s’agit de (it is a matter of, it’s about)
Il s’agit de (it is a matter of, it’s about) is a fixed expression that introduces the
subject of a work (book, film, etc.) or of a situation.

     De quoi s’agit-il?                      What is it about?
      Dans ce film, il s’agit d’un crime.            This film is about a crime.
      Dans ce roman, il s’agit d’une princesse.     This novel is about a princess.
         Il s’agit de ton avenir.                     Your future is at stake.
