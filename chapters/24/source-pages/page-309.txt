  The indefinite relative pronouns ce qui, ce que, ce à quoi, and ce dont are
frequently placed at the beginning of a sentence to stress a point. This construction
compensates for the English intonation that is much more marked. When a verb
requires a preposition, it is repeated in the second clause.

     Ce qui est amusant, c’est les marchés aux      Flea markets are fun!
          puces.
     Ce qu’elle adore, c’est acheter des vieilles     She loves buying old chairs!
           chaises.
