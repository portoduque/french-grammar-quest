  The relative pronouns qui and que can sometimes be followed by the subjunctive.
If there is a doubt about the existence of someone or the possible realization of
anything, the subjunctive may be used after the relative pronoun.

          Il cherche un artisan qui puisse   He is looking for an artisan who might be

  When the antecedent of the relative pronoun qui or que is a superlative such as le
plus (the most), le moins (the least), le seul (the only), l’unique (the unique), le
premier (the first), le dernier (the last), etc., the subjunctive may be used.
