Expressing duration
Remember: If you are asking a question about the duration of an action that began in
the past and still continues in the present, you have several options: depuis, il y a...
que, or cela (ça) fait... que, used along with the duration. Note that French uses a
present tense verb whereas English uses the past.
   Let’s review depuis. To ask a question about the duration of an action, use
Depuis quand? (Since when?) or Depuis combien de temps? (How long?)
