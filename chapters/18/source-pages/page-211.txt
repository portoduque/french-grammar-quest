The sequence of tenses: la concordance des temps
If the verb in the main clause is in the present tense, the verb in the dependent clause
remains in the same tense as it is in the direct speech.

       Paul dit: « Ce n’est pas important. »  Paul says: “It’s not important.”
       Paul dit que ce n’est pas important.   Paul says it is not important.
