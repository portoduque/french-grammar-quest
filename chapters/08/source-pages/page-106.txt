   Let’s look at the different uses of the imparfait. It is used for background and
description. It describes a situation that existed in the past, a state of mind or being.

      Les rues étaient embouteillées.         The streets were jammed.
      La circulation était fluide.             The traffic was flowing.
          Il faisait trop chaud.                            It was too hot.
          Il avait faim.                    He was hungry.
       Elle ne savait pas quoi faire.           She did not know what to do.

The imparfait versus the passé composé
As they express a mental or physical state of being, some verbs tend to be used more
often in the imparfait than in the passé composé. Among these verbs are: être (to
be), avoir (to have), penser (to think), croire (to believe), savoir (to know), espérer
(to hope), sembler (to seem), paraître (to appear). However, when these verbs are
used in the passé composé, they may take on a different meaning.

          Il semblait déprimé.              He looked depressed.
       Tout à coup il a semblé comprendre la  Suddenly he seemed to understand th
           situation.                                  situation.
       Je savais qu’il avait raison.                I knew he was right.
      Immédiatement, j’ai su qu’il était       Immediately, I realized he was
          innocent.                               innocent.
