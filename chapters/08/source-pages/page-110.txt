The imparfait with special constructions
With a si + on construction, the imparfait is used to make a suggestion or to invite
someone to do something. The informal on refers to two or more people and is
conjugated in the third-person singular.

       Si on allait en France cet été?    What about going to France this summer?
       Si on achetait des billets?       What about buying tickets?
       Si on allait nager dans le lac?    What about going swimming in the lake?
       Si on allait rendre visite à Léo?  What about paying a visit to Léo?
