  The imparfait is also used to describe a continuous action that was going on in
the past when another action (expressed in the passé composé) interrupted it.

       Elle regardait la télévision quand       She was watching television when
         soudain elle a entendu un grand bruit.    suddenly she heard a loud noise.
          Il faisait ses devoirs quand son frère est  He was doing his homework when h
          arrivé.                                 brother arrived.

  To express the idea that an action had been going on for a period of time before
being interrupted, use the imparfait with depuis. This is the equivalent to the past of
depuis + present tense you studied in Chapter 1.
