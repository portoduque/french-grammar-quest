       The imperative mood




The imperative mood (l’impératif) is used to give orders, make suggestions, or give
advice.

Formation of the imperative
To make the three forms of the imperative, take the tu, nous, and vous forms of the
present tense. For the -er verbs, drop the -s of the present tense tu form.

       parler to speak
        Parle!                          Speak!
       Parlons!                            Let’s speak!
        Parlez!                         Speak!

        choisir to choose
        Choisis!                           Choose!
        Choisissons!                             Let’s choose!
        Choisissez!                        Choose!

       répondre to answer
       Réponds!                         Answer!
      Répondons!                             Let’s answer!
       Répondez!                        Answer!

       boire to drink
        Bois!                           Drink!
