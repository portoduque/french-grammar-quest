Irregular imperative forms
Some imperatives are irregular. Let’s take a look at être, avoir, and savoir.

        être to be
        sois                               be
       soyons                                          let’s be
       soyez                              be

       avoir to have
        aie                         have
       ayons                                  let’s have
       ayez                        have

        savoir to know
