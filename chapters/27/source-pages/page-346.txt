Avoir beau and quitte à
The expression avoir beau + infinitive can usually be translated by although or
however.

         Il a beau être aisé, il n’est pas heureux.   Although he has a lot of money, he
                                                  not happy.
         Il a beau dormir, il est toujours fatigué.  However much he sleeps, he is
                                              always tired.
         Il avait beau habiter au bord de la mer, il Although he lived by the sea, he
          n’a jamais appris à nager.                never learned how to swim.
       Elle aura beau essayer, ce sera inutile.   However much she tries, it will be
                                                        useless.

The expression quitte à + infinitive can usually be translated by even if it means.

       Je vais lui dire ce que je pense, quitte Even if it means arguing with her, I am
        à me fâcher avec elle.               going to tell her what I think.
       Quitte à payer plus, je préfère       Even if it means paying more, I prefer
         voyager en première classe.            traveling first class.
       Je n’accepterai pas leur offre, quitte à  I won’t accept their offer even if it mea
          être renvoyé.                       being fired.
       Quitte à s’ennuyer, ils préfèrent rester They prefer to stay home even if it mea
         chez eux.                           being bored.
