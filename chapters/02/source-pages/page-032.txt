  Type 2 -ir verbs drop the -ir of the infinitive, then add the appropriate ending,
without the -iss- in the nous and vous forms. These groups of -ir verbs can be
termed irregular. Let’s look at the examples below:

        sortir to go out
         je sors            I go out             nous sortons        we go out
        tu sors        you go out           vous sortez          you go out
          il/elle sort     he/she goes out          ils/elles sortent        they go out
