The numbers 0 to 50
Let’s start with numbers from 0 to 50. Although consonants are generally silent in
French, they are pronounced in the following numbers: cinq, six, sept, huit, neuf,
dix. With sept, the -p- is silent, but the final -t is pronounced. The final -x in six and
dix is pronounced like an s.
  When the numbers cinq, six, huit, and dix are followed by a word beginning with
a consonant, their final consonant is mute (silent).
