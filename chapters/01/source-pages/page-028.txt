One more use of the present tense: depuis
The present tense is used to express an action that began in the past and continues in
the present. Note that in English, the past tense is used. There are different ways to
formulate the questions, using either depuis, il y a... que, cela (ça) fait... que.
   Let’s start with depuis. To ask a question about the duration of an action, use
depuis quand (since when) or depuis combien de temps (how long).
