The placement of adjectives
As you continue studying French, you’ll become familiar with the irregular feminine
forms of certain adjectives. What is often more difficult is knowing where to place
adjectives. In French, most qualificative adjectives follow the noun.

       Carole aime les plats italiens.          Carole loves Italian dishes.
       Danielle préfère la cuisine chinoise.     Danielle prefers Chinese cooking.
          Il porte des chaussures noires.       He is wearing black shoes.
       C’est un remède efficace.               This is an efficient remedy.
