In French, comparisons of adjectives and adverbs can take three forms, plus... que
(more . . . than), moins... que (less . . . than), aussi... que (as . . . as). For example:

     Ce modèle est plus récent que le   This model is more recent than mine.
         mien.
     New York est plus grand que San New York is bigger than San Francisco.
          Francisco.
      Marc est moins riche que Paul.   Mark is less rich than Paul. / Mark is not a
                                              rich as Paul.
      Luc est aussi célèbre que Bertrand.Luc is as famous as Bertrand.

Comparison of nouns
To compare quantities, use the following expressions. Note the use of de (d’) with
expressions of quantity:

       Elle a plus de temps que    She has more time than Valérie.
           Valérie.
          Il a moins de chance que toi. He is less lucky than you are. / He is not as lucky
                                     as you.
       Elle a autant de jouets que  She has as many toys as you do.
             toi.
