        J’ai mal à la tête.                          I have a headache.
          Il a mal au bras.                   His arm hurts.

  When avoir du mal à is followed by a verb,  it means to have trouble doing
something.

      Nous avons du mal à accepter sa      We have trouble accepting his
           décision.                                  decision.
       Elle a du mal à monter l’escalier.       She has trouble climbing the stairs.
