The -oir verbs
The -oir verbs do not all follow the same conjugation pattern. We’ll start with the
very useful verb savoir (to know). Savoir means to know a fact, to know how to do
something from memory or study.

        je sais             I know             nous savons         we know
        tu sais         you know           vous savez            you know
         il/elle sait      he/she knows           ils/elles savent          they know

       Elle sait jouer du piano.        She can play the piano.
          Il sait ce poème par cœur.      He knows this poem by heart.
         Ils savent gérer leurs affaires.   They know how to manage their business.

  Note that savoir is sometimes translated by can. Before a dependent clause or
before an infinitive, only savoir can be used.

       Je ne sais pas où il est.                       I don’t know where he is.
          Il sait qu’elle est occupée.          He knows she is busy.
