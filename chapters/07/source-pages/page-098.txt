Pronominal verbs in the passé composé
As we mentioned  earlier,  all pronominal verbs are conjugated with  être. The
reflexive pronouns precede the  auxiliary verb  (être). In most  cases, the  past
participle agrees in gender and number with the subject of the pronominal verb.
