 10. Tu (assister) ________________ à la pièce de théâtre.


  Remember that the past participle of regular -ir and -re verbs is formed by
dropping the infinitive endings -ir and -re and adding the appropriate ending -i or -u.

        finir               to finish                  fini             finished
       choisir            to choose               choisi         chosen
      vendre            to sell              vendu          sold
      perdre            to lose              perdu            lost

   In the passé composé of verbs conjugated with avoir, the past participle agrees
with the direct object of the verb, but only in sentences where the direct object noun
or pronoun precedes the verb. For example:

          Il a pris la bonne décision.        He made the right decision.
          Il l’a prise.                   He made it.
       Elle a compris ses erreurs.          She understood her mistakes.
       Elle les a comprises.               She understood them.
