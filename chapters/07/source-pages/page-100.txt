Six verbs among those conjugated with être in the passé composé (sortir, rentrer,
monter, descendre, passer, retourner) are conjugated with avoir and follow the
avoir agreement when a direct object follows the verb. In these cases, the meaning of
the verb has changed.

         Ils sont montés en haut de la Tour Eiffel.They went to the top of the Eiffel Tow
         Ils ont monté les malles au grenier.    They took the trunks up to the attic.

  Note above that les malles is the direct object of monter, thus it is conjugated
with avoir.
