The partitive article
The partitive article is used when the exact quantity of an item is unknown. In
English, the partitive article is often omitted. We say, “I want bread” or “I want some
bread.” However, the partitive article is always required in French. It is formed by
combining de and the definite article.

      de + le = du
      de + l’ = de l’
