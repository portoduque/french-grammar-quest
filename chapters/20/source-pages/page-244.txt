How to say from
From is expressed by de (d’) for continents, feminine countries, provinces, regions,
and states. For masculine and plural entities, the definite article is retained (du, des).

       Elle est rentrée de Suède hier soir. She came back from Sweden last night.
          Il n’est pas encore revenu d’Italie. He is not yet back from Italy.
       Elle est originaire du Brésil.      She comes from Brazil.
       Je reviens du Sénégal.               I am back from Senegal.
      Le vol arrive des États-Unis.     The flight is arriving from the United State
