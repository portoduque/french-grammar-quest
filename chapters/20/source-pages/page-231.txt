The gender of nouns
Noun endings often indicate gender. However, you cannot rely solely on these
patterns, as there are exceptions. We’ll look at few of them at the end of this section.
  Nouns ending in -age, -eau, and -ment tend to be masculine.

        le fromage                        cheese
        le barrage                   dam
        le courage                       courage
        le voyage                               trip
        le gâteau                        cake
        le plateau                           tray
        le tableau                          painting
        le château                           castle
        le compliment                   compliment
        le monument                  monument
        le sentiment                         feeling
        le paravent                        screen (furniture)

  Nouns ending in -t, -al, -ail, -eil, -isme also tend to be masculine.
