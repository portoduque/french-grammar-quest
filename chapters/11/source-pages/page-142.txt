The past conditional
The passé du conditionnel (past conditional) expresses what would have happened
if another event had taken place, or if certain conditions had or had not been present.
It is formed with the present conditional of être or avoir and the past participle of
the main verb. The rules of agreement common to all compound tenses still apply.

      donner to give
        j’aurais donné   I would have given  nous aurions donné we would have
                                                                   given
        tu aurais donné you would have     vous auriez donné  you would have
                           given                                   given
          il/elle aurait     he/she would have   ils/elles auraient     they would have
         donné           given            donné              given

       venir to come
