# Chapter 11 — The present conditional and the past conditional

## 1. The Present Conditional (Le présent du conditionnel)

The **présent du conditionnel** (present conditional) has many uses in French. It is formed by adding the endings of the imperfect (**imparfait**) to the future stem (**radical du futur**) of the verb:

$$\textbf{Radical du futur simple} + \textbf{Terminaisons de l'imparfait} \ (-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient)$$

- For regular **-er** and **-ir** verbs, the future stem is the entire infinitive form (*parler-* ➔ *je parlerais*, *finir-* ➔ *tu finirais*).
- For **-re** verbs, drop the final **-e** from the infinitive before adding the conditional endings (*mettre* ➔ *mettr-* ➔ *je mettrais*, *prendre* ➔ *prendr-* ➔ *elle prendrait*).
- Irregular verbs use the exact same irregular future stems (seen in Chapter 9):
  - *avoir* ➔ **aur-** (*j'aurais*)
  - *être* ➔ **ser-** (*tu serais*)
  - *faire* ➔ **fer-** (*il ferait*)
  - *aller* ➔ **ir-** (*nous irions*)
  - *pouvoir* ➔ **pourr-** (*vous pourriez*)
  - *savoir* ➔ **saur-** (*tu saurais*)
  - *vouloir* ➔ **voudr-** (*ils voudraient*)
  - *devoir* ➔ **devr-** (*elle devrait*)
  - *venir* ➔ **viendr-** (*nous viendrions*)

### Conjugation Models

#### Mettre (*to put*)
| Pronom Sujet | Forme Conjuguée | Traduction Anglaise |
|---|---|---|
| **je** | mettrais | I would put |
| **tu** | mettrais | you would put |
| **il / elle / on** | mettrait | he / she / one would put |
| **nous** | mettrions | we would put |
| **vous** | mettriez | you would put |
| **ils / elles** | mettraient | they would put |

#### Faire (*to do / make*)
| Pronom Sujet | Forme Conjuguée | Traduction Anglaise |
|---|---|---|
| **je** | ferais | I would do |
| **tu** | ferais | you would do |
| **il / elle / on** | ferait | he / she / one would do |
| **nous** | ferions | we would do |
| **vous** | feriez | you would do |
| **ils / elles** | feraient | they would do |

## 3. Uses of the Present Conditional

### A. Wishes and Suggestions (Souhait et Suggestion)
The présent du conditionnel is used to express a wish, a desire, or a gentle suggestion:
- *Je **voudrais finir** ce projet aussitôt que possible.* (I would like to finish this project as soon as possible.)
- *À ta place, je **parierais** sur ce cheval.* (If I were you, I would bet on this horse.)
- *Ce **serait** génial de pouvoir y aller ensemble.* (It would be great to go together.)
- *Il **aimerait rencontrer** la femme de sa vie.* (He would like to meet the woman of his dreams.)

### B. Politeness and Requests (Politesse et Inversion)
The présent du conditionnel is used to make a statement or request more polite, often using subject-verb inversion:
- *« **Pourriez-vous** nous donner votre avis ? »* (Could you give us your opinion?)
- *« **Voudriez-vous** dîner avec nous ce soir ? »* (Would you like to have dinner with us this evening?)
- *« Est-ce que tu **pourrais** me donner un coup de main ? »* (Could you give me a hand?)
- *« Est-ce que tu **voudrais** assister à la réunion ? »* (Would you like to attend the meeting?)

### C. Condition Implied with « SI » (L'Hypothèse Potentielle)
When a condition is implied, the conditional describes the potential consequence:
$$\textbf{Si} + \textbf{Imparfait}, \ \textbf{Conditionnel Présent}$$
*(ou : Conditionnel Présent + si + Imparfait)*

- *Nous **irions** à Rome **si nous pouvions**.* (We would go to Rome if we could.)
- *Régis **finirait** son roman **s'il pouvait** trouver une maison d'édition.* (Régis would finish his novel if he could find a publisher.)
- *Nous **aurions** un chien **si notre appartement était** plus grand.* (We would have a dog if our apartment were bigger.)
- *Nous **viendrions** **si notre baby-sitter était** disponible.* (We would come if our babysitter were available.)

> [!WARNING]
> In French, **never** put the conditional directly after *si*!
> Rule: *« Les SI n'aiment pas les -RAIS ! »*
> ❌ *Si j'aurais le temps...* ➔ ✅ **Si j'avais le temps, je viendrais.**

### D. The Journalistic Conditional (Le Conditionnel Journalistique)
Used in newspapers, TV, and radio broadcasts to express unconfirmed or alleged information (*reportedly*, *allegedly*):
- *La reine de Suède **se rendrait** en Australie la semaine prochaine.* (The Queen of Sweden is reportedly going to Australia next week.)
- *Son beau-frère **serait impliqué** dans une affaire de fraude fiscale.* (His brother-in-law is allegedly involved in tax fraud.)
- *Le président **signerait** le traité en fin d'après-midi.* (The president will reportedly sign the treaty by the end of the afternoon.)
- *Un sous-marin hollandais **serait** au large des côtes bretonnes.* (A Dutch submarine is reportedly off the coast of Brittany.)

### E. Formal French : Savoir = Pouvoir
In formal or literary French, **savoir** in the présent du conditionnel functions as an elegant equivalent of **pouvoir** (*can*, *to be able to*):
- *Je ne **saurais** vous dire combien j'apprécie votre geste.* (I shall never be able to tell you how much I appreciate your gesture.)
- *Je ne **saurais** vous répondre.* (I am afraid I can't answer you / I wouldn't know how to answer you.)

## 4. The Past Conditional (Le passé du conditionnel)

The **passé du conditionnel** expresses what would have happened in the past if another event had taken place, or if certain conditions had or had not been present.

### Formation
$$\textbf{Auxiliaire AVOIR ou ÊTRE au Conditionnel Présent} + \textbf{Participe Passé}$$

- Most verbs use **avoir** (*j'aurais parlé*).
- Movement verbs (Maison d'Être) and all pronominal/reflexive verbs use **être** (*je serais allé(e)*, *nous nous serions vus*), with agreement in gender and number.

#### Donner (*to give*, with Avoir)
| Pronom Sujet | Forme Conjuguée | Traduction Anglaise |
|---|---|---|
| **j'** | aurais donné | I would have given |
| **tu** | aurais donné | you would have given |
| **il / elle / on** | aurait donné | he / she / one would have given |
| **nous** | aurions donné | we would have given |
| **vous** | auriez donné | you would have given |
| **ils / elles** | auraient donné | they would have given |

#### Venir (*to come*, with Être)
| Pronom Sujet | Forme Conjuguée | Traduction Anglaise |
|---|---|---|
| **je** | serais venu(e) | I would have come |
| **tu** | serais venu(e) | you would have come |
| **il / elle / on** | serait venu(e) | he / she / one would have come |
| **nous** | serions venu(e)s | we would have come |
| **vous** | seriez venu(e)(s) | you would have come |
| **ils / elles** | seraient venu(e)s | they would have come |

### More Examples
- *Elle **aurait accepté** votre offre.* (She would have accepted your offer.)
- *Nous **aurions été** déçus.* (We would have been disappointed.)
- *Tu **aurais ri** !* (You would have laughed!)
- *Ils **se seraient mis** en colère.* (They would have gotten angry.)

## 5. Uses of the Past Conditional

### A. Regret or Reproach (Regret et Reproche)
- *Nous **aurions voulu** y assister.* (We would have liked to attend.)
- *Tu **aurais dû** arriver plus tôt.* (You should have arrived earlier.)
- *Elle **aurait aimé** le féliciter.* (She would have liked to congratulate him.)
- *Cela **aurait été** tellement plus facile.* (It would have been so much easier.)

### B. The Unreal Past Hypothesis (Hypothèse Irréelle du Passé)
The passé du conditionnel is usually paired with a *si* clause in the **plus-que-parfait**:
$$\textbf{Si} + \textbf{Plus-que-parfait}, \ \textbf{Conditionnel Passé}$$
*(ou : Conditionnel Passé + si + Plus-que-parfait)*

- *Elle **aurait fini** plus tôt **si vous l'aviez aidée**.* (She would have finished earlier if you had helped her.)
- *Je **serais arrivé** à l'heure **s'il y avait eu** moins de circulation.* (I would have arrived on time if there had been less traffic.)
- *Si le patron **n'avait pas voté** la délocalisation, l'usine **serait restée** ouverte.* (If the boss had not opted for relocation, the factory would have stayed open.)
- *Il **n'aurait pas tant gagné** d'argent **s'il était resté** à Bordeaux.* (He would not have made as much money if he had stayed in Bordeaux.)

### C. The Past Journalistic Conditional (Conditionnel Journalistique Passé)
Used for unconfirmed past news (*reportedly*, *allegedly*):
- *Le tremblement de terre **aurait fait** des milliers de victimes au Mexique.* (The earthquake reportedly killed thousands of people in Mexico.)
- *Le président l'**aurait gracié**.* (The president reportedly granted him a pardon.)
- *Ce célèbre chanteur français **se serait installé** en Suisse.* (This famous French singer reportedly moved to Switzerland.)
- *Il **aurait volé** la voiture de son voisin.* (He allegedly stole his neighbor's car.)

## 6. The Expression « Au cas où » (In Case)

Both the **présent** and **passé du conditionnel** are used with the conjunctive expression **au cas où** (*in case* / *na hipótese de que*):

$$\textbf{Au cas où} + \textbf{Conditionnel (Présent ou Passé)}$$

- *« **Au cas où** le projet **ne serait pas fini** cet après-midi, appelez-moi. »* (In case the project is not finished by this afternoon, call me.)
- *« **Au cas où** ce cadeau **ne vous plairait pas**, dites-le-moi. »* (In case you do not like this present, let me know.)
- *« **Au cas où** il **aurait échoué** à l'examen, on lui donnera une autre chance. »* (In case he failed the exam, we'll give him another chance.)
- *« **Au cas où** tu **n'aurais pas retrouvé** tes clés, Christian s'occupera de toi. »* (In case you did not find your keys, Christian will take care of you.)
