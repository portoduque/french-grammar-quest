The causative form
The causative form is, in most cases, used to express the idea of having something
done by someone or of causing something to happen. It is formed with the verb faire
followed by an infinitive.

       Elle écrit la lettre elle-même.         She writes the letter herself.
       Elle fait écrire la lettre par sa        She has the letter written by her
           secrétaire.                              secretary.
         Ils envoient le document.            They send the document.
         Ils font envoyer le document.        They have the document sent.
       Elle fait la robe.                   She is making the dress.
       Elle fait faire la robe.               She is having the dress made.
