The immediate future tense
Aller is also used to form the immediate future. So, to talk about what you are going
to do, use aller in the present indicative followed immediately by a verb in the
infinitive.

       Je vais acheter une voiture en mai.       I am going to buy a car in May.
      Nous allons faire un voyage en avril.  We are going to go on a trip in April.
