The passé simple of irregular verbs
Other verbs have an irregular passé simple. For some verbs the stem of the passé
simple is based on the past participle, but this is not a fixed rule. Needless to say,
when it comes to irregular verbs, everything is a bit more complicated, particularly
the passé simple. For example, the transformation, if you will, of a regular infinitive,
such as parler (to speak), into a passé simple form, such as Elle parla (she spoke),
is no stretch of the imagination. But when we wander into the thicket of irregular
verbs, everything becomes topsy-turvy. For instance, the switch from the infinitive
être (to be) to a passé simple form is a quantum leap: Aliénor d’Aquitaine fut une
reine hors du commun. (Eleanor d’Aquitaine was an extraordinary queen).

third-person singular and plural forms.
